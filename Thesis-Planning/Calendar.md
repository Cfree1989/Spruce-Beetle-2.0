# Spring 2027 thesis calendar

Prepared October 26, 2026, for a master’s thesis defended and uploaded in Spring 2027. You are an LSU employee, so the days below split into three kinds:

- **Graduate School deadlines** you have to hit.
- **Employee holidays** for LSU and A&M College, from [Permanent Memorandum 5](https://www.lsu.edu/administration/policies/pmfiles/pm-05.pdf) (revised August 28, 2026). These are paid days off.
- **Student breaks.** Classes are out. PM-5 still has you scheduled to work. Faculty often leave anyway, so treat those weeks as bad times to need a committee.

PM-5 allows the president to add a holiday if the governor declares one. That happened for January 2, 2026. Recheck PM-5 before you leave for winter closure. If your appointment is on a campus other than LSU A&M, use that campus’s table in the same memo. Some of those tables take Memorial Day and skip Mardi Gras.

## Admin checklist

Dates are the Graduate School dates. Ask the graduate coordinator whether the department wants the draft or the defense earlier.

- [x] Meet with your advisor or graduate coordinator. Walk the degree audit and Program of Study: courses done, research hours, thesis hours, GPA above 3.0, anything older than five years at graduation, transfer credit if you have it.
- [ ] Confirm the committee with your chair and the members: at least three, chair from the major department, at least one full member of the graduate faculty. Email is enough for now. The Graduate School form is the [Request for Master’s Defense and Degree Audit](https://www.lsu.edu/graduateschool/students/files/student_forms/rqst_for_masters_defense_and_deg_audit_fillable_new.pdf), due with the defense date. Someone who is not LSU graduate faculty also needs a CV sent with that form, several weeks earlier. Ask the graduate coordinator if the department wants its own notice before that.
- [ ] Register for spring, including 8000. Scheduling opened October 26. The add deadline is January 21 at 4:30 p.m.
- [x] Read the [Thesis & Dissertation Handbook](https://www.lsu.edu/graduateschool/students/theses_and_dissertations.php) and start the document in that format.
- [ ] Ask each committee member for spring availability and how long they need with a draft. Do this before winter closure (last regular work day is Wednesday, December 23).
- [ ] Agree a defense date. The scheduling math is in the next section.
- [ ] Submit the Application for Degree and save the confirmation in [Submissions/](Submissions/). The form has been open since September 5. The deadline is January 22 at 4:30 p.m.
- [ ] Submit the Request for Final Defense at least three weeks before that date, and no later than January 22 at 4:30 p.m. Save the confirmation.
- [ ] After the defense: committee revisions, formatting, upload by March 25 at 4:30 p.m.
- [ ] Clear editor corrections by April 16 at 4:30 p.m. Save the approval message.

Research and writing milestones are not on this list yet. The windows they have to fit are at the bottom.

## Scheduling consequences

These are calculated from the dates above. They are targets to take to your committee, not a defense that is already booked.

The defense request has two clocks, and the earlier one wins:

- It has to reach the Graduate School at least three weeks before the defense.
- It also has to be in by Friday, January 22, at 4:30 p.m., even if the defense is later.

If the request goes in on January 22, the earliest defense is three weeks later: **Friday, February 12**. Filing before winter closure moves that earlier. Filing on December 23 would allow a defense as early as January 13, the first week of spring classes. That only helps if the thesis is already ready.

The Graduate School’s own advice is to defend about two weeks before the upload so revisions and formatting still fit. Two weeks before March 25 is Thursday, March 11, which is spring break. A defense the committee will actually attend is more likely in the last full class week before that break.

**Friday, March 5** leaves 20 days before the upload, including the five days of spring break (you are at work; they may not be) and the week of March 15. A defense on Monday, March 15 leaves 10 days, and the day after the upload is a university holiday.

Put the date on the request before you leave on December 23 if the committee will give you one. After you return on January 4 there are **14 work days** until the January 22 deadline, and one of them is missing: Monday, January 18, MLK Day, is in that last week. The add deadline for 8000 hours is the day before the form deadline.

Good Friday is the day after the upload. The first regular work day after the file is in is Monday, March 29. From March 29 through the April 16 correction deadline there are **15 work days**.
